# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This code applies to everyone who contributes to WeAreB repositories, issues,
pull requests, and related discussion channels.

## Standards

Expected behaviour:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may edit, remove,
or reject contributions and comments that do not meet them.

## Reporting

Report concerns to [devteam@weareb.co](mailto:devteam@weareb.co). Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not act in good faith may face
consequences determined by organisation leadership.

## Enforcement

Consequences for violations range from a private warning to a permanent ban from
all organisation repositories and channels, decided by the maintainers based on
severity and recurrence.
